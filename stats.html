How it counts: when the app opens online it adds +1 to a few plain numbers on the free Abacus counting service. No cookies, no names, no IDs and no ads. Reloads within 30 minutes count once. “Different devices” is counted the first time the app is opened on a phone or browser, so clearing the browser data counts it again. Opens while offline are not counted.
